# Outlet commercial document collections

Restaurants owns Invoice and CreditNote history; CashSession does not own these collections.
The existing table uses `ORG#{orgId}#OUT#{outletId}#DOCS#{YYYYMM}` with
`INV#{issuedAt}#{invoiceId}` and `CN#{validatedAt}#{creditNoteId}`. Timestamps and
month buckets use UTC, with millisecond ISO timestamps generated by the server.

Publication FINAL and CreditNote validation atomically write compact projections.
The active BusinessDay pointer and OPEN day are condition-checked in that transaction;
no active day is represented by null and a conditional absence check. Neither Payments
nor calendar dates determine the association. No CashSession is required for CreditNote.

CreditNote locator: canonical CreditNote PK, `SK=DOCUMENT_LOCATOR`. It stores the exact
monthly projection key. Refund uses that locator and conditionally replaces the projection
balances in its existing transaction. Historical CreditNotes without locators retain their
existing Refund behavior and receive no synthetic projection.

Coverage begins with writes after activation. There is no backfill, write-on-read, Scan,
or assertion that old documents are exhaustively covered. Known historical detail paths
continue to work. Invoice digest payloads are unchanged.

Collection routes reuse get-outlet with checkout-read authorization plus existing
OWNER/MANAGER/CASHIER outlet checks. Requests require UTC from/to instants, at most
31 elapsed days. Queries visit relevant monthly partitions newest first, evaluating at
most 500 rows per request. A continuation can accompany zero matches. Cursors bind
scope, actor, kind, dates, BusinessDay, normalized search and page size. No totals or
decorative counters are generated. Detail reads are deferred until an action opens.

Invoice search covers technical invoiceId and table/service snapshot. CreditNote search
adds AV reference and reason. Dates are explicit filters; invoiceReference is not generated.
